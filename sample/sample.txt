Main recently large language models, voice agents, aur real-time communication systems ke baare mein kaafi padh raha hoon. Especially mujhe yeh samajhna interesting lagta hai ki ek voice assistant user ki speech ko kitni jaldi process karke meaningful response generate kar sakta hai.

For example, agar main bolun, “What’s the weather in New York today?” toh ideal system ko pehle speech recognize karni hogi, phir intent samajhna hoga, aur uske baad correct answer generate karna hoga. Agar poora process 5 ya 6 seconds le raha hai toh user experience kaafi slow feel hoga. Lekin agar response 1 second ke andar aa jaye, toh conversation much more natural lagti hai.

Waise accuracy bhi equally important hai. Agar user ne bola “book a flight from Delhi to Mumbai” aur system ne “book a hotel” samajh liya, toh latency fast hone ka koi fayda nahi hai. Real-world applications mein dono cheezein balance karni padti hain.
